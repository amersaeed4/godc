## Advanced Laparoscopic Cancer & Obesity Surgery

The Advanced Laparoscopic Cancer & Obesity Surgery Centre is the only specialist center in Lahore providing personalized weight management solutions along with cancer and other advanced laparoscopic procedures. A highly experienced team of Bariatric Surgeons, General Surgeons, Gastroenterologist, Endocrinologists, Anesthesiologist, Clinical Psychologists, Specialist Dietitians and Interventional Radiologist work with you to help you in the management of various surgical issues with different modalities.

### Advanced Laparoscopic Surgery

- Lap Cholecystectomy
- Lap Appendectomy
- Lap. Inguinal Hernia (TAPP)
- Lap. Ventral Hernia Repair
- Lap. Fundoplication
- Lap. Varicocelectomy
- Lap Needle Herniotomy
- Total Laparoscopic Hysterectomy
- Lap. Ovarian Cystectomy
- Lap. Duodenal Ulcer perforation repair
- Lap. Nephrectomy
- Lap. Cystogastrostomy for pseudopancreatic cyst
- [Read more about advanced laparoscopic procedures](/our-services/alcos/advanced-laparoscopic-surgery)

### Cancer Surgery

- Breast Cancer (MRM)
- Thyroid Cancer
- Gastric Cancer
- Esophageal Cancer
- Colorectal Cancer
- Pancreatic and Periamullary Cancer

### Obesity Management Services

**Surgical**

- Laparoscopic Sleeve Gastrectomy
- Laparoscopic Roux-En-Y Gastric Bypass
- Laparoscopic Mini Gastric Bypass
- Laparoscopic Gastric Banding

**Non-Surgical**

- Endoscopic Ballooning
- Angiographic Guided Gastric Artery Embolization
- Diet & Exercise
