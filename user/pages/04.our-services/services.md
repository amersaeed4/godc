    title: ICU NUTRITION (ENTERAL / PARENTERAL)
    text: Critically ill patients need precise nutritional support at every stage of intensive care. We design and monitor enteral and parenteral feeding plans that protect lean mass, support organ function and aid recovery.
    why: Our critical care team calculates, delivers and adjusts feeding daily alongside your treating consultants and ICU staff.
  - category: Clinical & Critical Care Nutrition
    title: Sepsis & Trauma Nutrition
    text: Sepsis and major trauma trigger rapid muscle breakdown and heavy metabolic demand. We provide protein and energy focused feeding strategies that stabilise metabolism, reduce complications during the acute phase.
    why: We match nutrition delivery to each phase of illness, from resuscitation through to active rehabilitation and recovery.
  - category: Clinical & Critical Care Nutrition
    title: Post-Surgical Nutrition
    text: Recovery after surgery depends heavily on adequate protein, energy and micronutrient intake. We prepare pre and post operative nutrition plans that support wound healing, reduce infection risk and shorten hospital stay.
    why: We work directly with surgical teams to plan feeding before admission and continue support after discharge.
  - category: Clinical & Critical Care Nutrition
    title: Organ Failure Nutrition Support
    text: Liver, kidney, cardiac and respiratory failure each demand carefully adjusted nutrition. We build individualised plans that balance protein, fluid, sodium and electrolytes while meeting energy needs without overloading failing organs.
    why: Our plans are reviewed against live laboratory results so nutrition remains safe as organ function changes.
  - category: Clinical & Critical Care Nutrition
    title: TPN (Total Parenteral Nutrition)
    text: When the gut cannot be used safely, intravenous nutrition becomes essential. We formulate, deliver and monitor total parenteral nutrition with close attention to line safety, and timely transition to oral feeding.
    why: Every TPN prescription is compounded, monitored and adjusted daily under close specialist medical and pharmacy supervision.
  - category: Obesity & Metabolic Health
    title: Obesity
    text: Obesity is a complex medical condition influenced by hormones, behaviour, environment and genetics. We provide structured, medically supervised weight management combining nutrition therapy and ongoing clinical monitoring.
    why: We treat obesity as a medical condition, not a willpower problem, with evidence based clinical care.
  - category: Obesity & Metabolic Health
    title: Metabolic Syndrome
    text: Raised blood pressure, blood sugar, waist circumference and abnormal lipids often occur together. We address these linked risk factors with one coordinated plan that improves metabolic health and lowers long term cardiovascular risk.
    why: One integrated plan targets every component of the syndrome instead of treating each abnormal result separately.
  - category: Obesity & Metabolic Health
    title: Insulin Resistance
    text: Insulin resistance often develops silently years before diabetes appears. We use targeted assessment, dietary restructuring, activity prescription and sleep and stress support to protect long term metabolic health.
    why: Early detection allows us to reverse insulin resistance before it progresses into established type 2 diabetes.
  - category: Obesity & Metabolic Health
    title: Dyslipidemia
    text: Abnormal cholesterol and triglyceride levels are a major driver of cardiovascular disease. We combine dietary fat quality, weight management and lifestyle change with medical review to bring your lipid profile within target.
    why: Nutrition therapy works alongside your prescribed medication to improve lipid results and overall cardiovascular protection.
  - category: Obesity & Metabolic Health
    title: NAFLD / Fatty Liver Disease
    text: Fatty liver disease is common, often silent and largely reversible in its early stages. We deliver structured nutrition, gradual weight reduction and lifestyle change to lower liver fat, inflammation and fibrosis risk.
    why: We track liver enzymes and body composition together to confirm real, measurable improvement over time.
  - category: Cardiometabolic Disorders
    title: Hypertension
    text: High blood pressure rarely causes symptoms but quietly damages the heart, brain and kidneys. We deliver sodium, potassium, weight and stress focused therapy alongside medical care to achieve sustained blood pressure control.
    why: Our lifestyle programmes are designed to support, not replace, the medication your physician has prescribed.
  - category: Cardiometabolic Disorders
    title: Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus
    text: Living with type 1 diabetes requires precise, flexible daily decisions around food and insulin. We provide carbohydrate counting, activity planning that improve glucose stability without limiting normal life.
    why: We teach practical skills that let you adjust confidently around meals, exercise, travel and illness.
  - category: Cardiometabolic Disorders
    title: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
    text: Type 2 diabetes can often be improved, and sometimes reversed, with intensive lifestyle therapy. We combine structured nutrition, physical activity, weight reduction and glucose monitoring to achieve fewer complications.
    why: Our remission focused programmes are medically supervised, with safe, planned adjustment of glucose lowering medication.
  - category: Cardiometabolic Disorders
    title: Prediabetes
    text: Prediabetes is a clear warning sign and a valuable opportunity to change direction. We provide early, structured intervention that restores normal glucose levels and significantly reduces your risk of developing type 2 diabetes.
    why: Acting at this stage is the most effective and least invasive point to protect your health.
  - category: Cardiovascular Diseases
    title: Coronary Artery Disease (CAD)
    text: Narrowed coronary arteries restrict blood flow to the heart muscle and increase cardiac risk. We provide heart focused nutrition, activity and risk factor management that supports arterial health and reduces future cardiac events.
    why: Our cardiac nutrition plans follow current international guidelines and are tailored to your medical history.
  - category: Cardiovascular Diseases
    title: Rehabilitation
    text: Recovery after a cardiac event, stent or bypass surgery needs structure, supervision and confidence. We deliver graded activity guidance, nutrition therapy and risk factor control to help you return safely to daily life.
    why: Rehabilitation is paced to your capacity and reviewed regularly as your strength and stamina improve.
  - category: Cardiovascular Diseases
    title: Stroke (CVA)
    text: After a stroke, nutrition supports brain recovery, swallowing safety and prevention of further events. We provide texture appropriate meal planning, blood pressure and lipid management, and practical support for everyday eating.
    why: We coordinate with neurology and therapy teams so nutrition supports your wider rehabilitation goals throughout.
  - category: Cardiovascular Diseases
    title: Peripheral Vascular Disease
    text: Reduced circulation to the legs causes pain, slow healing and steadily reduced mobility. We address the underlying vascular risk factors with targeted nutrition, weight management, activity guidance and dedicated wound healing support.
    why: Improving circulation and healing capacity helps protect limb function, comfort and long term independent mobility.
  - category: Specialized Nutrition
    title: Oncology Nutrition
    text: Cancer and its treatment often affect appetite, weight, taste and physical strength. We provide nutrition support that preserves lean muscle, manages treatment side effects and helps you recovery.
    why: Our plans adapt around chemotherapy, radiotherapy and surgery schedules to keep intake consistent throughout treatment.
  - category: Specialized Nutrition
    title: Gastrointestinal Disorders
    text: Digestive conditions can make eating unpredictable, restrictive and genuinely uncomfortable. We use structured, to identify triggers, correct nutrient deficiencies and rebuild a varied diet you can tolerate confidently.
    why: We avoid unnecessary long term restriction and work steadily towards reintroducing foods wherever it is safe.
  - category: Specialized Nutrition
    title: Stress-induced Gastritis
    text: Stress, irregular eating and medication use can inflame the stomach lining and cause persistent discomfort. We combine meal timing, dietary modification and stress management to calm symptoms and support mucosal healing.
    why: We treat the digestive symptoms and the daily habits, timings and stressors that drive them.
  - category: Specialized Nutrition
    title: Liver Disease Nutrition
    text: Liver disease alters how the body processes protein, salt, fluid and essential micronutrients. We design plans that maintain muscle mass, manage fluid retention and support liver function at every stage of illness.
    why: Nutrition is adjusted as liver function changes, with regular clinical, biochemical and body composition review.
  - category: Specialized Nutrition
    title: Endocrine Disorders (PCOS, Thyroid)
    text: Hormonal conditions affect weight, energy, cycles, skin and mood in ways diet strongly influences. We provide targeted nutrition and lifestyle therapy that improves hormonal balance, metabolic markers and everyday symptom control.
    why: We address the hormonal and metabolic drivers together, rather than focusing on weight alone.
  - category: Specialized Nutrition
    title: Pediatric Nutrition
    text: Children have changing nutritional needs that shape growth, immunity, learning and lifelong eating habits. We provide age appropriate assessment and family centred plans for growth concerns, and medical conditions.
    why: Plans are practical for families, protecting healthy growth without creating pressure or conflict at mealtimes.
  - category: Geriatric & Palliative Care Nutrition
    title: Frailty & Age-related Malnutrition
    text: Reduced appetite, dental problems and illness often cause unintentional weight loss in older adults. We provide enriched, easy to eat nutrition plans that restore strength, energy, immunity and daily independence.
    why: We screen for malnutrition early and use realistic, appealing food based solutions before considering supplements.
  - category: Geriatric & Palliative Care Nutrition
    title: Cachexia
    text: Cachexia causes severe muscle and weight loss that ordinary eating alone cannot reverse. We provide intensive nutritional and metabolic support to slow decline, preserve strength and maintain comfort and quality of life.
    why: Our goals are set honestly and compassionately, focusing on comfort, function and realistic clinical benefit.
  - category: Geriatric & Palliative Care Nutrition
    title: End-of-life Nutritional Support
    text: In advanced illness, the purpose of nutrition shifts from treatment towards comfort and dignity. We provide gentle, individualised guidance that respects patient wishes and supports families through difficult and emotional feeding decisions.
    why: We prioritise comfort, choice and dignity, supporting both the patient and the wider family throughout.
  - category: Geriatric & Palliative Care Nutrition
    title: Dysphagia Management
    text: Difficulty swallowing increases the risk of choking, chest infection and poor nutritional intake. We provide texture modified meal planning, safe feeding techniques and ongoing monitoring to protect both nutrition and airway safety.
    why: Our plans follow recognised texture standards and are reviewed with speech and language therapy input.
  - category: Geriatric & Palliative Care Nutrition
    title: Functional Decline & Muscle Loss
    text: Loss of muscle strength reduces mobility, balance and confidence, raising the risk of falls. We combine higher protein nutrition, vitamin D support and resistance based activity to rebuild strength and functional capacity.
    why: Progress is measured through practical strength, mobility and body composition changes rather than weight alone.

    text: Healthy change is about improving body composition, not simply lowering the number on the scale. We provide structured nutrition, activity and behavioural support that reduces fat mass while protecting valuable lean muscle.
    why: We measure fat, muscle properly so your progress reflects real, lasting physical change.
  - category: Aesthetic & Preventive Nutrition
    title: Skin Health & Anti-Aging Nutrition
    text: Skin quality reflects hydration, blood sugar control, inflammation and overall micronutrient status. We provide targeted nutrition strategies that support collagen production, reduce inflammatory triggers and improve skin clarity.
    why: We treat skin from the inside out, correcting internal deficiencies alongside your dermatology or aesthetic care.

    title: Hair Loss & Micronutrient Therapy

    why: Supplementation is guided by laboratory testing, never guesswork, and reviewed regularly for safety and effectiveness.

    why: We offer clinically sound wellness programmes rather than fad detoxes, crash plans or unproven products.

    title: Stroke Rehabilitation
    text: Neurological recovery is supported by consistent nutrition, safe swallowing and controlled vascular risk factors. We provide structured plans that support brain repair, restore strength and lower the risk of further stroke.
    why: Nutrition therapy is coordinated with physiotherapy and speech therapy to support your full recovery programme.
  - category: Neurological Disorders
    title: "Dementia (e.g., Alzheimer’s Disease)"
    text: Dementia often affects appetite, mealtime routines, food recognition and the ability to eat independently. We provide practical nutrition strategies that maintain weight, hydration and dignity while supporting carers and families.
    why: We provide realistic mealtime guidance for carers, reducing daily stress while improving overall nutritional intake.
  - category: Neurological Disorders
    title: "Parkinson’s Disease"
    text: "Parkinson’s affects swallowing, digestion, medication timing and body weight in ways nutrition directly influences. We plan protein timing around levodopa, manage constipation and support stable weight, strength and safer mobility."
    why: Meal timing is planned carefully around your medication schedule to help maintain consistent symptom control.
  - category: Neurological Disorders
    title: Neuropathy (especially diabetic)
    text: Nerve damage causes pain, numbness and reduced sensation, particularly in the feet and hands. We combine strict glucose control, targeted micronutrient therapy and foot care guidance to slow progression and ease symptoms.
    why: Stabilising blood glucose remains the single most effective way to protect and preserve remaining nerve function.
  - category: Neurological Disorders
    title: Epilepsy (Nutrition-sensitive cases)
    text: Selected forms of epilepsy respond well to medically supervised ketogenic and modified dietary therapies. We assess suitability, initiate treatment safely and monitor closely to support seizure control and full nutritional adequacy.
    why: Ketogenic therapy is delivered only under neurology supervision, with careful monitoring of growth and bloods.
  - category: Renal Disorders
    title: Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)
    text: Kidney disease requires careful control of protein, sodium, potassium, phosphate and daily fluid intake. We provide stage specific plans that protect remaining kidney function, prevent malnutrition and delay progression toward dialysis.
    why: Plans are adjusted at every stage of kidney disease, guided by your latest laboratory results.
  - category: Renal Disorders
    title: Diabetic Nephropathy
    text: When diabetes affects the kidneys, glucose and kidney focused nutrition must work carefully together. We balance blood sugar control with protein, management to protect kidney function and cardiovascular health.
    why: We manage diabetes and kidney disease as one condition, avoiding plans that conflict with each other.
  - category: Renal Disorders
    title: Dialysis Nutrition (Hemodialysis / Peritoneal)
    text: Dialysis significantly changes daily protein, fluid, potassium and phosphate requirements. We provide tailored plans for haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis that prevent malnutrition, control fluid gain and wellbeing.
    why: Nutrition is matched to your dialysis modality, weekly schedule and most recent blood results.
  - category: Musculoskeletal Disorders
    title: Osteoarthritis
    text: Joint pain, stiffness and reduced movement are strongly influenced by body weight and inflammation. We combine weight management, anti-inflammatory nutrition and joint friendly activity to reduce pain and improve daily mobility.
    why: Even modest weight reduction meaningfully lowers joint load, easing pain and improving everyday movement.
  - category: Musculoskeletal Disorders
    title: Rheumatoid Arthritis
    text: Autoimmune joint inflammation affects energy, appetite, bone health and long term physical function. We provide anti-inflammatory nutrition, bone protection and muscle preserving strategies that complement your prescribed rheumatology treatment plan.
    why: Nutrition therapy supports your rheumatology treatment and helps manage medication related nutritional side effects.
  - category: Musculoskeletal Disorders
    title: Sarcopenia
    text: Age related muscle loss gradually reduces strength, balance, metabolism and personal independence. We combine higher protein intake, vitamin D correction and e training to rebuild muscle mass and functional strength.
    why: Nutrition and resistance training are prescribed together, because muscle only rebuilds when both are present.
  - category: Musculoskeletal Disorders
    title: Osteoporosis
    text: Weakened bone increases fracture risk long before any symptoms or warning signs appear. We provide calcium, vitamin D, protein and weight bearing activity plans that protect bone density and reduce fracture risk.
    why: Bone protection is planned alongside fall prevention, because both together determine your true fracture risk.
  - category: Musculoskeletal Disorders
    title: Chronic Pain Syndromes
    text: Persistent pain is influenced by inflammation, sleep quality, stress, activity levels and nutritional status. We provide an integrated plan addressing each of these factors to reduce pain intensity and restore daily function.
    why: We address the whole picture, including sleep, stress and nutrition, rather than treating pain alone.
