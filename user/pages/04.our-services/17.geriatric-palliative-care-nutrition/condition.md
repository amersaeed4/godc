signs:
  title: Early Signs of Geriatric & Palliative Care Nutrition
  text: |-
    Changes in nutritional needs often begin with a reduced appetite, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, or difficulty eating. Older adults and individuals with chronic illnesses may also experience muscle weakness, dehydration, or poor energy levels.

    Some people may find it difficult to chew or swallow food, while others may struggle to maintain a balanced diet due to illness or medication side effects. These changes can affect overall health, recovery, and daily functioning.

    Recognizing these early signs and seeking professional nutritional support can help prevent malnutrition and improve quality of life. A personalized nutrition plan can promote comfort, maintain strength, and support overall well-being.
