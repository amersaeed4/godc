signs:
  title: Early Signs of Musculoskeletal Disorders
  text: |-
    Early signs of musculoskeletal disorders often begin with mild pain, stiffness, or discomfort in the muscles, joints, or bones. These symptoms may appear after physical activity or gradually worsen over time.

    Some individuals may notice swelling, reduced flexibility, muscle weakness, or difficulty performing everyday movements such as walking, bending, or lifting. Joint tenderness and decreased range of motion are also common early indicators.

    Recognizing these symptoms early and seeking medical evaluation can help prevent further damage. Timely diagnosis and appropriate treatment can improve mobility, relieve pain, and support long-term musculoskeletal health.

causes:
  title: Causes of Musculoskeletal Disorders
  intro: Musculoskeletal disorders can develop due to a variety of factors that affect the bones, muscles, joints, ligaments, and tendons. Common causes include aging, repetitive movements, injuries, poor posture, and underlying medical conditions. Identifying these causes early can help prevent complications, reduce pain, and support long-term musculoskeletal health.
  items:
    - title: Repetitive Strain
      text: Repeated movements and overuse of muscles or joints can place excessive stress on the musculoskeletal system. Over time, this may lead to inflammation, pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility.
    - title: Injury & Trauma
      text: Falls, sports injuries, accidents, or fractures can damage bones, muscles, ligaments, and tendons. Proper treatment and rehabilitation are essential to restore function and prevent long-term complications.
    - title: Aging & Degeneration
      text: As the body ages, bones, joints, and muscles naturally lose strength and flexibility. This gradual wear and tear can increase the risk of conditions such as osteoarthritis, osteoporosis, and chronic joint pain.

risks:
  title: "What Increases the Risk of Musculoskeletal Disorders?"
  intro: |-
    Several factors can increase the risk of developing musculoskeletal disorders, including aging, obesity, repetitive physical activities, poor posture, lack of exercise, and previous injuries. Certain medical conditions, such as arthritis and osteoporosis, along with occupations that involve heavy lifting or prolonged sitting, can also place additional stress on the muscles, bones, and joints.

    Understanding these risk factors and adopting healthy habits can help reduce the likelihood of musculoskeletal problems.
  items:

  title: "How is Musculoskeletal Disorders Diagnosed?"
  intro: Diagnosing musculoskeletal disorders begins with a detailed review of your medical history, symptoms, and a physical examination.
