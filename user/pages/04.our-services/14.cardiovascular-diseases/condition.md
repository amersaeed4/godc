signs:
  title: Early Signs of Cardiovascular Diseases
  text: |-
    Cardiovascular diseases often begin with mild symptoms that may be mistaken for everyday fatigue or stress. Early warning signs can include chest discomfort, shortness of breath, dizziness, or unusual tiredness during routine activities.

    Some people may notice a rapid or irregular heartbeat, swelling in the legs or ankles, or pain that spreads to the arms, neck, jaw, or shoulders. These symptoms can develop gradually or appear suddenly.

    Recognizing these early signs and seeking medical attention promptly can help prevent serious complications. Early diagnosis and appropriate treatment are essential for protecting heart health and improving long-term outcomes.

causes:
  title: Causes of Cardiovascular Diseases
  intro: Cardiovascular diseases can develop due to a combination of genetic, lifestyle, and medical factors that affect the heart and blood vessels. Poor diet, physical inactivity, smoking, high blood pressure, and diabetes are among the most common causes. Identifying these risk factors early can help prevent complications and support long-term heart health.
  items:
    - title: High Blood Pressure
      text: High blood pressure places extra strain on the heart and blood vessels, increasing the risk of heart disease and stroke. Managing blood pressure through medication and healthy lifestyle choices is essential for cardiovascular health.
    - title: Unhealthy Lifestyle
      text: Poor eating habits, lack of physical activity, smoking, and excessive alcohol consumption can damage the heart over time. Adopting healthy daily habits can significantly reduce the risk of cardiovascular diseases.
    - title: High Cholesterol
      text: High cholesterol can lead to the buildup of plaque inside the arteries, restricting blood flow to the heart. Regular health checkups, a balanced diet, and proper treatment can help maintain healthy cholesterol levels.

    Several factors can increase the risk of developing cardiovascular diseases, including high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, obesity, smoking, physical inactivity, unhealthy eating habits, and chronic stress.   title: "How Are Cardiovascular Diseases Diagnosed?"
  intro: Diagnosing cardiovascular diseases begins with a detailed review of your medical history, symptoms, and a physical examination.
