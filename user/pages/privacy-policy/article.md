## Our commitment to your privacy

GODC – Gillani Obesity and Diabetes Clinic ("GODC", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

When you complete a booking, contact or feedback form we collect the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, age, gender, preferred appointment time and any message or symptoms you choose to describe.

## How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange and confirm appointments, provide clinical and administrative support and, where you have agreed, to share news and updates from GODC. We do not sell your personal information.

## Sharing and protection

Your information is accessed only by authorised GODC staff and service providers who help us operate this website and our clinic. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

## Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you updates, by contacting us using the details on our contact page.

## Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
