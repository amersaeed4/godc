## Preparing for Your Visit

We understand that visiting a healthcare facility can feel overwhelming, but our dedicated team at GODC is here to make your experience as comfortable and stress-free as possible. We are committed to providing compassionate care, clear guidance, and personalized support throughout your healthcare journey.

If you have any questions before your appointment, please feel free to contact our team. Our healthcare professionals are always available to provide the information you need and ensure you feel confident and prepared for your visit.

Please remember to bring any relevant medical records, laboratory reports, imaging results, referral letters, and a list of your current medications. Having this information available allows our specialists to provide a comprehensive assessment and develop the most appropriate treatment plan for your needs.

If you have any questions before your visit, please don’t hesitate to contact our healthcare team. You can reach us using the details on our contact page. Our friendly staff will be happy to answer your questions and assist you with your appointment.

## About GODC

GODC is committed to providing a safe, comfortable, and welcoming environment for every patient. Our facility is equipped with accessible entrances and patient-friendly amenities to ensure a smooth healthcare experience.

If you are unable to attend your scheduled appointment, please inform our team as early as possible so we can assist with rescheduling and make your appointment available for other patients.

To maintain a healthy environment for everyone, smoking is prohibited throughout the facility. We also request that mobile phones be kept on silent mode and used responsibly in designated areas.

## Points to Remember

### Insurance

If your treatment is covered by your health insurance provider, please contact your insurer before your appointment to obtain any required authorization or approval. This will help ensure a smooth billing process. Self-paying patients may be asked to complete payment during registration according to the selected services.

### Planning Your Return Home

Depending on the type of treatment or procedure you receive, you may require assistance when returning home. If your healthcare provider advises that you should not drive after your appointment, please arrange for a family member or friend to accompany you.

### What to Bring

To help us provide the best possible care, please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance information (if applicable), referral letters, previous medical records, laboratory reports, imaging results, and a list of your current medications.

We recommend wearing comfortable clothing and arriving a few minutes before your scheduled appointment to complete any necessary registration.

### Valuables

Please avoid bringing large amounts of cash, expensive jewellery, or other valuable personal belongings to your appointment. While GODC takes every reasonable precaution, we cannot accept responsibility for personal items that are lost or left unattended.
