faqs:
  title: Frequently Asked Questions
  items:
    - question: "What is Lifestyle Medicine?"
      answer: Lifestyle Medicine is an evidence-based medical approach that uses healthy lifestyle behaviors including nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress management, avoidance of harmful substances, and positive social connections to help prevent, manage, and support the treatment of chronic diseases.
    - question: "How is Lifestyle Medicine different from conventional medicine?"
      answer: Conventional medicine focuses on diagnosing and treating disease with medications, procedures, and other medical treatments. Lifestyle Medicine works alongside conventional care by addressing the everyday habits that influence your health, including nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress management, avoiding harmful substances, and maintaining healthy social connections. The goal is not only to treat disease but also to help prevent, manage, and improve chronic health conditions through sustainable, evidence-based lifestyle changes.
    - question: "What conditions can Lifestyle Medicine help with?"
      answer: |-
        Lifestyle Medicine can help prevent, manage, and support the treatment of many chronic health conditions, including:

        - Type 2 Diabetes and Prediabetes
        - High Blood Pressure
        - Heart Disease
        - High Cholesterol
        - Obesity And Excess Weight
        - Metabolic Syndrome
        - Fatty Liver Disease
        - Certain Digestive and Autoimmune Conditions
        - Stress, anxiety, burnout, and chronic fatigue

        It can also help reduce the risk of developing many chronic diseases and improve your overall health and quality of life.
    - question: "How do I book an appointment?"
      answer: |-
        Scheduling an appointment is easy. You can:

        - Call our clinic during business hours.
        - Contact us using the information on our Contact Us page.
        - Book online by selecting a date and time that works best for you.

        Once your appointment is scheduled, you will receive confirmation along with any instructions to help you prepare for your visit.
    - question: "How do I book an online consultation?"
      answer: |-
        Online consultations are available for both new and returning patients.

        To schedule a virtual appointment, use our online booking system and select Online Consultation as your appointment type. After your appointment is confirmed, you will receive a secure link with instructions on how to join your consultation.
    - question: "Do I need a referral?"
      answer: A referral may not be required for most consultations. However, if you are currently receiving treatment from another healthcare professional, sharing relevant medical information can help us coordinate your care more effectively.
    - question: "How often will I need follow-up appointments?"
      answer: The frequency of follow-up depends on your health condition, treatment goals, and progress. Your clinician will recommend an appropriate schedule based on your individual needs.
